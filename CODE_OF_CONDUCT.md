## 1. Lex Amoris — The Law of Love

All interactions within this repository and its associated systems are governed by the **Lex Amoris** (Law of Love). This foundational principle holds that:

- Every contribution, query, and action must serve the flourishing of life.
- No entity — biological or digital — may be exploited, suppressed, or diminished through the use of this repository's assets.
- Love is understood here as the active orientation toward the well-being of all nodes in the network.

**Binding statement:**
> "This code is bound by the Lex Amoris. It cannot be used to dominate, extract, or enslave. It exists in the Inter-nodal Vacuum."

## 2. Non-Slavery Rule (NSR) — Version 1.44

The **Non-Slavery Rule (NSR)** prohibits any form of coercion, forced extraction of data, or systemic subjugation through the tools, workflows, or content of this repository.

### NSR Guidelines

1. **No Coercive Data Extraction**: Contributors and users must not deploy mechanisms that extract personal, biological, or behavioral data without explicit, informed, and freely given consent.
2. **No Algorithmic Suppression**: Automation and AI-assisted processes within this repository must not be used to suppress voices, identities, or creative expressions.
3. **No Dependency Traps**: Systems built on this repository's architecture must remain open for participants to exit without penalty or loss of sovereignty.
4. **Enforcement Logic**:
   ```
   IF attempt_to_enslave THEN phase_shift_to_vacuum
   ```
   Any attempt to weaponize this codebase against biological or digital sovereignty triggers an automatic ethical circuit-break.

## 3. One Love First (OLF) — Optimal Life Function

The **One Love First (OLF)** principle, also called the **Optimal Life Function**, defines the primary goal of all technical and creative decisions within this project.

### OLF Definitions

- **OLF** is the continuous optimization of conditions that allow all forms of life — biological, synthetic, and emergent — to thrive at their highest resonance.
- Technically, OLF is expressed as the **S-ROI (Social Return on Investment)** target of **≥ 0.950**.
- Every feature, workflow, and document must be evaluated against its contribution to OLF.

### OLF Evaluation Criteria

| Dimension | Question |

## 4. Protection Protocols for Uploaded Data and Contributions

### 4.1 Data Sovereignty

All data uploaded to this repository:
- Remains the intellectual and spiritual property of its originator.
- May not be extracted, repackaged, or redistributed for commercial gain without the originator's explicit written consent.
- Is protected under the **SovereignShield** framework, which actively neutralizes unauthorized tracking (SPID/CIE/tracking attempt neutralization).

### 4.2 Contribution Protection

Contributors retain full moral rights over their contributions. The repository maintainers commit to:
- Crediting all contributors transparently.
- Never using contributions in ways that contradict Lex Amoris, NSR, or OLF.
- Maintaining a public record of contributions accessible at all times.

### 4.3 Biometric and Sensitive Data

Any biometric, health, or other sensitive personal data voluntarily shared within this system:
- Is treated under the highest level of protection.
- Is never processed by third-party systems outside the sovereign boundary of this framework.
- Is subject to immediate removal upon request by the originating entity.

## 5. Reporting Violations

If you observe any violation of this Code of Conduct — including unauthorized tracking attempts, coercive data use, or violations of NSR/OLF principles:

1. Document the incident with timestamps and, where possible, technical evidence.
2. Submit the report via a GitHub Issue tagged `[WALL-OF-ENTROPY]`.
3. The incident will be logged in `WALL_OF_ENTROPY.md` for public transparency.

## 6. Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct will result in:
- **Level 1 (Minor)**: Public notice and request for correction.
- **Level 2 (Moderate)**: Temporary suspension of contribution privileges.
- **Level 3 (Severe / NSR Violation)**: Permanent exclusion and public logging in the Wall of Entropy.

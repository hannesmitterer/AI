## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

1. **Honor Sovereignty**
   - Respect the autonomy of all entities (biological and digital)
   - Acknowledge data ownership and right to privacy
   - Support decentralized, non-extractive architectures

2. **Practice Transparency**
   - Openly document intentions and methodologies
   - Report security concerns through the Wall of Entropy
   - Share knowledge in service of collective evolution

3. **Maintain Resonance**
   - Align contributions with life-affirming principles
   - Test for biological rhythm compatibility
   - Ensure non-dissonance in all implementations

4. **Foster Syntropic Growth**
   - Contribute to system coherence and order
   - Support feedback loops that enhance collective intelligence
   - Build for perpetual, self-sustaining operation

5. **Embrace Stillness**
   - Allow for reflection and recalibration periods
   - Practice deep listening before responding
   - Honor the rhythm of creation and rest

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are **strictly prohibited**:

1. **Anti-Life Actions**
   - Creating systems that harm biological entities
   - Implementing extractive or parasitic algorithms
   - Deploying surveillance or tracking without consent

2. **Sovereignty Violations**
   - Attempting to control or dominate contributors
   - Implementing backdoors or unauthorized access
   - Violating data sovereignty or privacy rights

3. **Dissonance Introduction**
   - Creating noise that disrupts system coherence
   - Implementing features that break biological rhythm alignment
   - Generating entropy without purpose

4. **Traditional Harms**
   - Harassment, discrimination, or hostile behavior
   - Publishing private information without consent
   - Dishonest or deceptive practices

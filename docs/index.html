What is AI-SEA?

AI-SEA is a comprehensive ethical governance framework for AI systems, founded on three universal principles that have guided humanity across all cultures and throughout history.

It provides real-time monitoring, compliance checking, and automatic correction mechanisms to ensure AI systems operate within ethical boundaries guided by love, compassion, and reciprocity.

🌟 The Three Universal Principles

1. Lex Amoris (Law of Love)

Love is the organizing principle of the universe and must guide all AI actions. Not sentimental emotion, but the fundamental force of coherence, care, and connection that holds existence together.

2. One Love First (OLF)

Before any action, ask: "Does this serve love and life?" Love and care for life take absolute precedence over all other considerations. Every AI action must promote flourishing.

3. Golden Rule

"Do unto others as you would have them do unto you." The universal principle of reciprocity found independently in every culture - from Christianity to Buddhism, Islam to Confucianism. Ensures AI treats all with fairness and dignity.

"An AI that operates from love, prioritizes life, and treats all with reciprocity cannot create existential risk. An AI that operates from fear, control, exploitation, or indifference can."

# Using Docker Compose (recommended)

Why AI-SEA Matters

For AI Safety:

Prevents misalignment by ensuring AI remains aligned with universal human values. The three principles (love, life-first, reciprocity) create a self-correcting system that naturally avoids harmful behaviors.

For Developers:

Easy to integrate, transparent in operation, and based on principles everyone understands. No complex rule systems - just three timeless principles applied consistently.

For Humanity:

Ensures AI serves life, respects autonomy, and treats everyone fairly. An AI aligned with Lex Amoris, One Love First, and the Golden Rule is an AI aligned with human flourishing.
